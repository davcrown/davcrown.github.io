About Me

Data, Basketball & Community

I'm a Senior at Rice University studying Sports Analytics and Statistics. My goal is to combine data science with my passion for sports, while maintaining civic service throughout my career.

As a Basketball Analytics Scout at SkillCorner, I was part of a small team producing high-quality datasets for analytics firms and professional teams. Deciphering the right annotations for in-game events taught me to operate in grey areas, problem-solve toward the goal at hand, and pay close attention to detail.

Today I lead the Technical Projects subteam of the Rice Sport Analytics Team, serve as a Lab TA for Rice Statistics, and co-host Against the Grain on KTRU Sports.
