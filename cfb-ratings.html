CFB BAYESIAN RATINGS

WEEKLY TEAM RATINGS FOR EVERY FBS AND FCS PROGRAM

A dynamic hierarchical Bayesian model that rates every FBS and FCS football team each week, with offense and defense estimated separately and adjusted for opponent strength.

Open Full Dashboard ↗ How It Works
266 FBS and FCS teams rated in 2026
43+ Weekly model fits, 2024 – 2026
6,000 Posterior draws per fit, across 4 chains
90% Credible intervals on every rating and rank

Win-loss records and polls say little about how good a team actually is, especially across conferences that rarely play each other. This model estimates each team's offense and defense separately, adjusts both for the strength of the opponents faced, and reports how uncertain each rating is.

It covers the 2024 through 2026 seasons and is refit every week during the season. Each weekly fit predicts the following week's games, and the dashboard scores those picks against the real results.

How the Model Works

01

A Three-Level Hierarchy

Teams sit inside conferences, and conferences inside tiers: Power 4, Group of 6, and FCS. Each team's strength follows a weekly random walk, so ratings can change as a season goes on.

02

Scores, Not Just Wins

A bivariate-normal model of both teams' scores, with a home-field advantage estimated for each team, fit in Stan with 4 chains and 6,000 draws per fit.

03

Tested Every Week

More than 43 fits, one per week of 2024–2026. Each fit predicts the following week, and the dashboard scores those predictions against the actual results.

Kept Up to Date

An Automated Weekly Pipeline

During the season, a pipeline pulls the latest results, fits the new week, and redeploys the app, so the ratings stay current without manual work.
