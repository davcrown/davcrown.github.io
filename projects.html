Winner · Spurs² Hackathon

Can We Use This Clip?

The winning submission at the Spurs² Hackathon at UT Austin, hosted by ScorePlay and TwelveLabs with Tottenham Hotspur and the San Antonio Spurs. My team of five built a rights and compliance engine that checks every clip against each partner's terms. I built the ScorePlay write-back: a clearance tag per rule sheet on every clip and timecoded evidence logs on videos. Partners' access is gated on those tags, so they see only what's cleared. I also built a contract parser that turns rights agreements into machine-checkable rules.

  • ScorePlay API
  • TwelveLabs

DSCI 305 · Data Ethics

RUPD Crime Log Dashboard

An R Shiny dashboard that turns 11 years of Rice University's public crime log — 32 semesters and 3,450 charges, partly recovered from the Internet Archive — into maps, charge breakdowns, time-of-day trends, semester comparisons, and case outcomes. Built to the NIST AI Risk Management Framework, with jittered locations and reporting-time data to protect the people behind each record.
